# Indian gaming Shorts and stream-highlight style guide (BGMI, Free Fire, GTA RP, Minecraft), 2025–2026

Method note (read first). All YouTube numbers below were pulled on **2026-11-05** from YouTube's own channel Shorts tabs and search results (via the built-in browser; YouTube's internal browse/search/player responses, not a third-party scraper). For each channel I took the **latest ~96 Shorts on the Shorts tab and sorted them by views** ("recent-top"), then fetched exact length/date/views for the top 6–8. This is not an all-time "Popular" sort, which is why some 2021–2023 Shorts still appear for channels that post rarely. Length buckets were computed on 258 Shorts with known lengths (135 of them published 2025-01-01 or later). For five channels I fetched lengths/views for all 96 recent Shorts to test what correlates with views on the same channel. Thumbnails were viewed as still images (no videos downloaded). Handles that failed to resolve: @ScoutOP, @KaztroGaming, @TwoSideGamers, @MortaL, @GodLikeZORO, @TripathiGaming, @AjjuBhai (wrong/unknown handles; not sampled). Anything marked *(inference)* is my reading of the sample, not a sourced fact.

## Key question 1: Length distribution of top gaming Shorts, and whether 3-minute Shorts are used

### Takeaway
On the sampled Indian gaming channels, the recent-top Shorts are still mostly 30–60 s (47% of 2025–26 top Shorts), but 61–180 s Shorts are now a real slice (21% of 2025–26 top Shorts) and, for several channels, the longest Shorts are also among the most viewed; sub-15 s "one-moment" clips are the third style (16%).

### Inferences
- *(inference)* For a clipping app the safe default is 20–45 s for a single kill/clutch moment and 60–120 s for a "full fight" or "last zone" clip; the data does not show a penalty for 61–180 s Shorts on the sampled channels, and several small Free Fire/BGMI channels have their best recent numbers on 90–175 s Shorts.
- *(inference)* Sub-15 s clips work when the moment is a single visible shock (car spray, headshot, funny glitch); they do not suit multi-step clutches.
- *(inference)* The cross-channel medians by bucket are heavily confounded by channel size (Mythpat/Dynamo pull the 61–180 bucket up), so treat the same-channel numbers as the better evidence.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube data on Indian gaming Shorts length vs retention was found; only vendor aggregators with conflicting figures.
- The "Popular" (all-time) sort of channel Shorts tabs could not be fetched; the sample is recent-96-sorted-by-views, which under-represents older viral Shorts on prolific channels.

## Key question 7: Which of these can GCM Clipper reproduce automatically (pipeline mapping)

### Takeaway
Most of the evidenced style is template-able: 9:16 crop with HUD kept, facecam-top split, a black-band hook caption in bold caps with one emoji, Hinglish stat/punchline titles, series naming, 20–45 s single-moment clips plus 60–120 s "last zone" clips, and a face-cut-out + big-text compilation thumbnail; what is not automatable from the evidence are the meme/reaction formats and knowing which off-game personality moment will land.

### Cited Findings
- Facecam detection and crop layouts, Hinglish captions, hook text, zoom on kills, titles and a weekly compilation are already in the app (per the assignment brief); the evidence above shows the matching conventions: facecam-top split — [Dynamo thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/KeoPg_NYbHc/oardefault.jpg), [Payal thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/dWpPf9pJi8A/oardefault.jpg); black-band hook — [V I N U thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/aEmLQrFj08A/oardefault.jpg), [Total Gaming thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/mjKB_BwI_2c/oardefault.jpg)
- Length targets are evidenced in Key question 1 (30–60 s majority; 61–180 s 21% of 2025–26 top Shorts; <15 s for single-shock moments) — channel Shorts tabs, seen 2026-11-05
- Title/hook vocabulary is evidenced in Key question 2 ("1v4", "last zone", "wait for end", "patt se headshot", "999 IQ", "POV:", "X ❌ Y ✅", "Rate this")
- Compilation packaging is evidenced in Key question 4 (8–13 min POV highlights; "[N] FINISHES | context | BGMI" titles; face cut-out + 2–3 word text thumbnails; no chapters)
- Hashtag convention for small channels: "#bgmi #bgmishorts #shorts #pubgmobile" or "#freefire #freefireshorts #shorts"; large channels omit hashtags — Short links in Key question 2

### Inferences
- *(inference)* Automatable now: (1) default 9:16 gameplay crop that keeps kill feed and minimap; (2) facecam-top 35–45% split when a cam is detected; (3) top black band with a 1–2 line hook in heavy condensed caps (white or yellow, dark outline), with the emoji drawn from a small set (😱 💀 🔥 🤣 😂 🤯); (4) title generator using stat/Hinglish/suspense templates plus a per-channel series counter; (5) two length presets (single moment 20–45 s; full fight 60–120 s) chosen by detected fight duration; (6) hashtag set by game and channel size; (7) lower-third creator branding (name, UPI/Instagram) as an optional burn-in; (8) compilation thumbnail from facecam frame cut-out + stat text; (9) compilation title "[N] FINISHES/KILLS | week | game".
- *(inference)* Needs a human or a later model: reaction/meme formats, off-game personality clips, picking which teammate roast is funny, and sound/music choice (no evidence on music vs original audio).
- *(inference)* Low-value to build from this evidence: velocity/transition edits, chapters in compilations, and "watch full stream" links in Short descriptions (top creators do not use them).

### Gaps
- No data on whether burned-in "watch full stream" text inside the video (as opposed to the description) is used or helps; descriptions alone show it is not a norm.
- Zoom/speed-ramp prevalence remains unverified (stills only).
