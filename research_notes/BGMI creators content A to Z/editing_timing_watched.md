# How the editors time their cuts and effects (watched in Chrome, 14 Nov 2026)

The owner watched Kaatclip's first highlights and said the editing "was not perfectly matching with game, audio
and movement". To see how real editors time things, three posted videos were played in Chrome and measured in the
page (frames compared between moments, loudness read while playing; nothing downloaded), with contact sheets at
0.25-2 s steps and the saved auto-captions for timing. Sound itself could not be heard.

## Goblin second channel, stream cut: "PMGC Grind with Old Teammates" (wDgeZBn80VM, 362 s)
- Inside a fight there is **no editing at all**: 65.8-74.2 s at 0.6 s steps is one continuous piece of the stream
  (camera whips, scope in and out, smoke, recoil). Of 75 frame jumps, most were the game's own camera moves (the
  face cam unchanged across them).
- The caption-matched map (research_notes/BGMI highlight channels deep study/goblin_deep.md, Map 1): the whole
  match became **4 stretches of 87, 26, 71 and 162 s**, each starting on the call ("idhar se gaadi aa raha hai")
  or the first shots and running straight through; the final fight kept whole through the "#2" screen plus 18 s of
  talk.
- No zooms, no face punch-ins, no sound effects, no subtitles, no text.

## JokerClips, comedy: "Joker Played Meccha Chameleon For The First Time With S8UL" (Kobcv7gzuAY, 483 s)
- 26 caption-matched units in 8 minutes (2-48 s): comedy cuts often, on the joke.
- **The face full screen is a hard cut**, held for the line or the reaction (23.25-25.5 s), then a hard cut back.
  No animated zoom.
- **A zoom into the game shows what the joke is about**: at 27.25 s a hard cut to a close-up of the toilet, half a
  second before the line "ye to tatti hai" (31.75 s caption, the run-up "jeet gaya ye game" at 27.75 s), held about
  1.5 s, with a reaction picture popping in at the corner halfway (28.0 s), then a hard cut back (29.0 s).
- So effects are **timed to what is said**, not to sound peaks.

## GoBLiN shorts, a Short: "50 Seconds of Bullying @SouLNakuL" (HhcQY2wm8Ug, 50 s, 456K views)
- The layout follows the moment: **game on top and face below while shooting; the face alone, big, over a blur
  while someone talks or reacts** (10-13 s, 24-28 s, 36-40 s); a teammate's reaction in black and white with a name
  label ("Nakul:", 30 s); one meme at the punchline (sunglasses over TV colour bars, 42 s).
- Switches are hard cuts on a sentence or a reaction, never in the middle of shooting.

## What Kaatclip did differently (and why it felt off)
- It chopped busy fights into a window around every kill (22 pieces in 8.9 min), so the game's movement was cut
  mid-motion; real cuts are few, long and start on the call or the first shots.
- It zoomed on every kill and added a boom, timed from the kill counter, which is read once a second and confirmed
  a read later, so they could land up to a second off the real kill; none of the channels does this.
- Face punch-ins went on loudness peaks, which are often gunfire, so the face cut in during shooting; real punch-ins
  are on talk or reactions, held for the sentence.
- Cuts were snapped out of words but not to the sound: a cut could fall in the middle of a burst of gunfire, with no
  fade.
