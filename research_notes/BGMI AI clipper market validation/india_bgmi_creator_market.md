# Indian BGMI and mobile-gaming creator market (as of late Oct 2026), customer sizing for AI-GCM

Research date: 2026-10-26. About 17 search/fetch calls. Many sources in this space are SEO blogs or vendor pages; reliability is flagged inline. Older data is marked with its year.

## 1. BGMI player base, ban history, Krafton India investment, esports

### Takeaway
BGMI is live and still Krafton's main India asset: 260M+ cumulative downloads and paying users up 17% YoY (Q1 2026). Krafton is putting more money into India, now planning about $500M in total. No official 2026 MAU figure is public, and Sensor Tower-based reporting says BGMI no longer dominates India's mobile-game charts the way PUBG Mobile did. The esports scene is active, with a 2026 peak-viewership record and a BGMI India Series 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Krafton Q1 2026 earnings: BGMI cumulative downloads passed 260 million and paying users rose 17% YoY (as reported). [Inside Sport](https://www.insidesport.in/bgmi/krafton-hits-records-q1-2026-revenue-bgmi-drives-strong-engagement-in-india/) (page returned 403 on fetch; figure taken from the search snippet). The same 260M figure is repeated in [Storyboard18, Krafton ad-tech arm enters India with BGMI, Apr 2026](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/brand-marketing/kraftons-ad-tech-arm-enters-india-launches-with-bgmi-108288.htm).
- Krafton plans to launch the lightweight "BGMI Lite" in India by late 2026, aimed at low-end devices and a wider audience. [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/24837/krafton-to-launch-lightweight-bgmi-lite-in-india-by-late-2026); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/gaming-news/krafton-bets-on-accessibility-with-bgmi-lite-aims-to-expand-gaming-audience-in-india-107942.htm)

[India Dispatch](https://indiadispatch.com/p/top-mobile-games-india)

[TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/19/krafton-hikes-india-bet-with-new-670m-fund). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/27/krafton-acquires-controlling-stake-in-indian-gaming-studio-nautilus-mobile-for-14m)

### Inferences
- With 260M cumulative downloads, rising paying users, BGMI Lite targeting low-end phones, and a $500M Krafton commitment, the platform risk of building a BGMI-specific tool looks lower in 2026 than it did in 2022–23. A second ban cannot be ruled out, though, so the product should not depend on one game.
- The India Dispatch piece suggests BGMI shares the battle royale audience with Free Fire MAX. A tool that handles both covers much more of the market.
- Peak esports viewership of about 0.73M shows a large spectator base. Esports orgs and casters who produce highlights could be a B2B segment.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 BGMI MAU or DAU was found. Krafton IR does not publish it, and the Sensor Tower article does not give one. Older third-party MAU figures (often quoted at 100M+) were not verified here.
- The exact 2023 unban date was not confirmed from a dated source. It is widely remembered as May 2023, but that is unverified.
- 2026 esports prize pools were not found.

## 2. How many Indian gaming creators/streamers exist; how many stream BGMI; size tiers

### Takeaway
No authoritative 2026 count of Indian gaming creators exists. The best available figures cover very different scopes: about 4.12M creators across all categories in India (ISB–Hashfame, 2025); a secondhand "300,000 gaming creators and streamers" (India Gaming Report 2024); and about 6,178 YouTube channels with "Gaming" in the bio and a mostly Indian audience (Modash directory). Only 8–10% of Indian creators monetize at all (BCG 2025). The gaming-creator funnel is therefore wide at the bottom, with a paying core in the low tens of thousands at most. That is my inference, not a sourced figure.

### Cited Findings
- India's creator base grew from about 0.96M (2020) to about 4.12M (2025), and 66% of creators come from non-metro markets. [ISB–Hashfame press release](https://www.isb.edu/news/press-releases/india-s-creator-economy-enters-a-new-phase-as-non-metro-creators-become-the-majority-reveals-isb-hashfame-report); [DAiOM summary](https://daiom.in/indias-4-12-million-creator-economy-isnt-what-you-think-it-is-blog/)
- BCG (2025, released at WAVES 2025): about 2–2.5M monetized creators, and only 8–10% of creators effectively monetize. Creators influence $350–400B of consumer spend a year, projected to pass $1T by 2030. [IBEF / BCG](https://www.ibef.org/news/india-s-creator-economy-projected-to-influence-over-us-1-trillion-rs-84-58-000-crore-in-consumer-spend-by-2030-bcg-report-to-be-unveiled-at-waves-2025); [Kotak MF](https://www.kotakmf.com/Information/blogs/inside-india-creator-economy). The two BCG figures do not reconcile cleanly; the report-writer should note this.
- "85% of creators do not make any money in a year," attributed to a CNBC-TV18 report (secondhand). [Pune Pulse](https://www.mypunepulse.com/indias-creator-economy-is-booming-but-most-creators-earn-little-beginners-guide-to-building-a-sustainable-career/)
- An India Gaming Report 2024 counted about 300,000 gaming creators and streamers. This is cited secondhand on a market-research page and the original was not accessed. The same page cites FICCI-EY: game-streamer viewership rose from 20% to 25%, especially in Tier-II cities. [Market.us](https://market.us/report/creator-economy-in-gaming-market/)
- Modash directory: 6,178 gaming YouTube influencers in India, defined as channels with "Gaming" in the description and a majority-Indian audience. This undercounts small creators and channels without that keyword. [Modash, Oct 2026](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/youtube/india/gaming)
- Top-tier examples: Techno Gamerz (about 46.7M subscribers) and Total Gaming (about 44.5–44.8M). Counts vary across lists. [Modash](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/youtube/india/gaming); [Infidigit](https://www.infidigit.com/blog/top-youtube-gaming-channels/). Well-known BGMI streamers include Payal Gaming and Jonathan Gaming. [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/bgmi/news-bgmi-star-payal-gaming-drops-hint-monthly-income); [Wikipedia: Jonathan Gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Gaming)
- Rooter says it has more than 85M registered users and hosts BGMI, Free Fire, Valorant, GTA V and COD Mobile streams. This is the company's own claim from promotional coverage, Oct 2026. [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/gaming/how-indias-game-streaming-platform-is-growing-its-gaming-community)
- Lumikai's latest report, State of India Interactive Media 2025 (5th edition): $13.8B interactive media economy growing 17% YoY; gaming time up 30% YoY to 13 hrs/week; the headline theme is the Indian user moving "from player to creator." [BW Marketing World](https://www.bwmarketingworld.com/article/india-s-interactive-media-economy-hits-13-8-bn-micro-dramas-are-leading-the-charge-603249); [Lumikai](https://www.lumikai.com/post/lumikai-launched-latest-research-report-from-swipes-to-stories-india-s-interactive-media-consumer). The earlier Lumikai and Google figure was $3.8B in FY24, forecast to reach $9.2B by FY29 (including RMG). [Digital in Asia](https://digitalinasia.com/india-gaming-market/); India gamers are cited at 591M. [Digital in Asia](https://digitalinasia.com/india-gaming-market/)

### Inferences
- These are rough sizing tiers, built by triangulation and not from any source:
  - Long tail: hundreds of thousands of Indian gaming streamers (the 300k figure), most earning nothing, so very low ability to pay.
  - Active semi-pro: thousands to low tens of thousands with monetized YouTube channels (Modash's 6,178 keyword-tagged channels is a floor).
  - Large: low hundreds with 1M+ subscribers. These creators usually already have editors or teams.
- BGMI is probably the largest single game among Indian mobile-gaming streamers, with Free Fire MAX a close rival, but no source gives a per-game split.
- The realistic serviceable market is creators who already earn something (Super Chat, sponsors, or YPP ads) and post Shorts. Applying BCG's 8–10% monetization rate to about 300k gaming creators gives roughly 24k–30k. This is a heuristic, not a sourced number.

### Gaps
- No primary source counts BGMI-specific streamers. Social Blade, Streams Charts, or Playboard category data (e.g., YouTube channels streaming BGMI per month) would be needed.
- No 2026 Lumikai "State of India Gaming" edition was found. KPMG, EY-FICCI 2026, Redseer and Kalaari gaming-creator breakdowns were not reached.
- No Google/YouTube India official gaming-creator figures were found.

## 3. Platforms: YouTube Live / Shorts, Instagram Reels, Loco, Rooter, Twitch; role of short-form

### Takeaway
YouTube (Live, VOD and Shorts) is the main home and monetization platform for Indian BGMI creators. Indian streaming apps have weakened: Loco cut 36% of staff in 2023 after its ad-supported model failed and shows no 2025–26 news, while Rooter still operates and claims 85M registered users. No evidence was found that Twitch exited India. Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are the discovery channels, which is exactly where AI-GCM's output would be posted.

### Cited Findings
- Loco laid off about 40 of 110 staff (about 36%) in Nov 2023. The founders shifted from ad-based to transaction-based monetization after the AVOD model failed. [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/2023/11/game-streaming-platform-loco-lays-off-40-employees/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/gaming-news/locos-avod-model-fails-will-it-bounce-back-37278.htm); [Esports Advocate](https://esportsadvocate.net/2023/12/loco-laysoff-nov302023/). Earlier funding was about $42M, including a $42M Series A in 2022 led by Hashed with Krafton and Lumikai participating. [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/09/indian-game-streaming-platform-loco-raises-42-million-from-hashed-makers-fund)
- Loco streamers earn through virtual goods bought by viewers. BGMI, Free Fire, GTA V and Valorant were its main titles (2022). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2282620)
- Rooter in Oct 2026: it positions itself as a live-streaming and esports platform with 85M+ registered users and streams of BGMI, Free Fire, Valorant, GTA V and COD Mobile (promotional source). [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/gaming/how-indias-game-streaming-platform-is-growing-its-gaming-community)
- Twitch: no India exit was found, and Twitch pays Indian Affiliates and Partners in USD. Its only documented country exit is South Korea (Feb 2024, network fees). [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/does-twitch-pay-in-india/); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2023/12/twitch-to-terminate-south-korean-operations-in-2024-due-to-high-network-fees)
- YouTube Super Chat is a core income source for Indian gaming live streams; see section 5. [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-super-chat-earnings-guide-india)
- Instagram's Edits app launched as a CapCut-style editor while CapCut remains unavailable in India. [Gizbot](https://www.gizbot.com/amphtml/apps/news/metas-new-edits-app-steps-in-to-let-you-create-and-share-videos-while-capcut-stays-unavailable-108361.html)

### Inferences
- YouTube is the centre of gravity, and Shorts and Reels are the main ways to grow and to funnel viewers into live streams. A tool that turns a 3–6 hour YouTube Live VOD into Shorts and Reels fits the dominant workflow. Supporting import from a YouTube Live URL is likely essential.
- Rooter and Loco streamers are a smaller, secondary segment. Rooter could be a partnership or distribution channel.

### Gaps
- No data was found on the share of Shorts views or subscriber growth from Shorts for Indian gaming channels, or on YouTube's India Shorts daily views.
- No 2025–26 status update for Loco (shutdown, pivot, or funding). STAGE was not researched; it appears to be a regional OTT service rather than gaming.
- No current Rooter MAU or streamer count from an independent source.

## 4. How creators currently make clips; editor costs; mobile and PC tools

### Takeaway
Clips are made by self-editing on phone apps (CapCut has been banned since 2020, so VN, InShot and Meta's Edits are the usual substitutes) or by paying freelance editors. Typical rates are ₹500–₹2,000 per Short or Reel, ₹2,900–₹14,000 per 10-minute long-form edit, and monthly retainers of ₹30k–₹1L+ covering about 8 long videos plus 20 Shorts. Full-time editor salaries are about ₹18k–₹25k/month. The tool's real competitor is a cheap human editor or the creator's own time.

### Cited Findings
- CapCut was banned in India in June 2020 alongside TikTok after the Galwan clashes. [Descript](https://descript.com/blog/article/capcut-ban-what-to-know-and-which-capcut-alternatives-to-consider)
- The common CapCut replacements are VN Video Editor (free, no watermark, multi-track), InShot (basic, for quick social clips) and Instagram Edits (no watermark, exports capped at HD). [Kripesh Adwani](https://kripeshadwani.com/capcut-alternative/); [Gizbot](https://www.gizbot.com/amphtml/apps/news/metas-new-edits-app-steps-in-to-let-you-create-and-share-videos-while-capcut-stays-unavailable-108361.html)
- Freelance YouTube edit (up to 10 minutes) as of Oct 2026: ₹4,764–₹14,290, median about ₹10,003; budget editors from about ₹2,900. This is a vendor's own page. [RaRe Frames](https://rareframes.io/faq/how-much-do-youtubers-pay-video-editors-in-india)
- Rates by channel size: small channels ₹500–₹2,000 per video, mid-size ₹5,000–₹10,000, top channels ₹25,000+. Undated guide. [The IoT Academy](https://www.theiotacademy.co/blog/salary-of-a-video-editor/)
- A basic Reel or Short costs ₹500–₹2,000. Monthly creator retainers run ₹30,000–₹1 lakh+, for example 8 long-form videos plus 20 Shorts. [Fueler 2026](https://fueler.io/blog/video-editor-salary-in-india-freelance-and-full-time-pay-guide)
- Freelance editors in India charge ₹647–₹3,882 per hour. [SoloHourly](https://solohourly.com/rates/video-editor-rates-in-india)
- Salaried video editors average ₹18,687/month on Indeed and about ₹25,000/month base on Glassdoor. [Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/career/video-editor/salaries); [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Salaries/video-editor-salary-SRCH_KO0,12.htm)

### Inferences
- Paying a freelancer for 20–30 Shorts a month costs about ₹10k–₹60k at ₹500–₹2,000 per Short. An AI tool at ₹499–₹1,999/month would be a 10–50x saving for creators who already pay editors.
- Creators who self-edit on free VN or InShot pay only with their time, so a free tier and a low entry price are needed to convert them.
- AI-GCM's differentiators would be finding highlights in long streams (finishes, clutches, chicken dinners) and Hinglish captions. Generic phone editors do neither automatically.

### Gaps
- No gaming-specific or BGMI-specific editor rate data. Rates for "clipping teams" (agencies that clip streams for top creators) were not found.
- YouTube Create usage in India was not covered by any source found.
- No survey of which tools Indian gaming creators actually use (e.g., PC tools such as Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve versus mobile apps).

## 5. Creator monetization and willingness to pay for SaaS; Indian creator-tool startups

### Takeaway
Indian gaming creators earn from Super Chat (the creator keeps about 70%), low ad RPMs (about ₹5–₹20 per 1,000 views), and sponsorships (about ₹50k–₹2L per video for mid-to-large channels). Most creators earn nothing (BCG: 8–10% monetize). Global clipping tools are priced at $15–$29/month (about ₹1,250–₹2,400), which is high relative to Indian earnings. No Indian gaming-focused AI clipping startup was found, which suggests open space but no local proof of demand.

### Cited Findings
- Super Chat: viewers pay ₹79–₹38,000 per message; the creator keeps about 70% and YouTube about 30%. A small, engaged audience can out-earn a larger casual one. SEO guide, lower reliability. [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-super-chat-earnings-guide-india)
- Live-stream earnings estimates: about ₹7,000–₹33,000 per stream for a 100k-subscriber channel, and ₹10,000–₹1,00,000+ per session for some creators. SEO guides. [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-live-stream-earnings-guide-india); [YTVerse](https://ytverse.in/monetize-youtube-gaming-channel-in-india-2026)
- Indian gaming YouTube RPM is about ₹5–₹20 per 1,000 views (one source gives a CPM of ₹10–₹50). [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-gaming-channel-india-2026); [YTVerse](https://ytverse.in/monetize-youtube-gaming-channel-in-india-2026)
- Sponsorships run about ₹50k–₹2L per video depending on size. Krafton itself sponsors BGMI creators, sometimes on monthly retainers. [YTVerse](https://ytverse.in/monetize-youtube-gaming-channel-in-india-2026)
- EY projects Indian influencer marketing at ₹3,375 crore in 2026. [Exif Media / EY](https://www.exifmedia.com/blog/creator-economy-india-2025-trends)
- Top earners: CarryMinati's net worth is estimated at about ₹50 Cr (unaudited). [Tring](https://www.tring.co.in/brands/richest-gamers-in-india). Payal Gaming declined to disclose her income. [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/bgmi/news-bgmi-star-payal-gaming-drops-hint-monthly-income)
- Global clipping-tool price benchmarks (USD): OpusClip Free $0, Starter $15/month, Pro $29/month or $174/year, at 1 credit per minute of source video (a 3-hour stream uses 180 credits). Klap is $29/month for up to 100 clips. [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/opusclip-pricing-plans-and-what-you-ll-actually-pay-in-2026); [Klap](https://klap.app/blog/opus-clip-pricing). No INR, PPP or UPI pricing from OpusClip was found.
- Adjacent startups: Clouted raised a $7M seed in May 2026 (Slow Ventures, Peak XV's Surge) for viral short-video clipping, but it is not confirmed as Indian or gaming-focused. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/clouted-wants-to-take-the-guesswork-out-of-making-short-videos-go-viral/). Indian video-AI companies include Clueso ($10M Series A, YC, business video), Dashverse ($13M Series A from Peak XV, Aug 2025, microdramas), Eloelo's Dolphin AI (FICCI FRAMES 2026) and Avataar (India-scale video AI). None is a gaming clipper. [Digit](https://www.digit.in/features/general/this-indian-ai-startup-wants-to-disrupt-video-production-workflows-heres-how.html); [TechCrunch Avataar](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/cheaper-faster-and-culturally-aware-avataars-video-ai-is-built-for-indias-scale/); [Kenerate](https://kenerateai.com/blogs/top-indian-ai-startups-for-video-and-image-ai-2026). InVideo, an Indian-founded company, is reported at about $70M ARR, mostly from global users. [ValueForStartups](https://valueforstartups.in/22-invideo-ai)

### Inferences
- Willingness to pay is concentrated in creators earning roughly ₹20k+/month from Super Chat or sponsors. For them, ₹500–₹1,500/month is under 5–10% of income and far cheaper than an editor. Likely price points: a free tier or trial with watermark and limited minutes; ₹299–₹499/month (Hobby); ₹999–₹1,499 (Pro); ₹2,999–₹4,999 for agencies and clipping teams. UPI AutoPay or Razorpay subscriptions and GST invoices are probably needed. These are inferences; no India creator-tool price survey was found.
- Per-minute pricing like OpusClip's is costly for Indian live streams, which typically run 3–6 hours. A flat "streams per month" or "hours per month" plan may convert better.
- InVideo's scale shows that Indian-founded creator SaaS can succeed, but mostly with global, USD-paying customers. An India-only, INR-priced gaming tool has no proven precedent.

### Gaps
- No primary data on Indian creators' spending on SaaS or on price sensitivity (no Redseer or Kalaari creator-tools survey found).
- No INR price lists were found for Indian creator tools (e.g., Pocket FM-style creator apps, Kinemaster India pricing, VN Pro).
- No Indian gaming-specific clipping startup was found. Esports highlight tools (e.g., an "esports highlights AI India" search) were not checked.

## 6. Adjacent market: Free Fire MAX, COD Mobile, other games; Hindi/Hinglish commentary

### Takeaway
Free Fire MAX is live in India and enforced actively (426,717 accounts banned for cheating after May 21, 2026), while the original Free Fire has been blocked since Feb 2022 and the "Free Fire India" relaunch never shipped. Garena dominates India's battle royale category alongside BGMI. No data was found on COD Mobile's India size or on the share of Hindi/Hinglish commentary, though the market's non-metro and Tier-II tilt points to heavy Hindi and regional-language use.

### Cited Findings
- Free Fire MAX is live. Garena's Free Fire India account reported 4,26,717 permanent bans since May 21, 2026. [Free Fire Keeda](https://freefirekeeda.in/news/free-fire-max-426717-accounts-banned-may-2026/)
- The original Free Fire was removed in Feb 2022. The India-specific relaunch announced in 2023 was postponed with no further progress. [SportsAdda](https://www.sportsadda.asia/esports/news-esports/free-fire-india-unban-date-pre-registration-steps-download/); [Inside Sport](https://www.insidesport.in/garena-free-fire/free-fire-max-ban-in-india-whats-the-truth/)
- Battle royale in India is "dominated by Garena, King, Voodoo, and others" (Sensor Tower via India Dispatch, Mar 2026). [India Dispatch](https://indiadispatch.com/p/top-mobile-games-india)
- Total Gaming (Free Fire, about 44.5M+ subscribers) is one of India's largest gaming channels, showing how big Free Fire's creator scene is. [Infidigit](https://www.infidigit.com/blog/top-youtube-gaming-channels/)
- 66% of Indian creators are non-metro (ISB–Hashfame 2025), and game-stream viewership is growing especially in Tier-II cities (FICCI-EY, cited secondhand). [ISB](https://www.isb.edu/news/press-releases/india-s-creator-economy-enters-a-new-phase-as-non-metro-creators-become-the-majority-reveals-isb-hashfame-report); [Market.us](https://market.us/report/creator-economy-in-gaming-market/)
- Rooter lists COD Mobile, Valorant and GTA V alongside BGMI and Free Fire. [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/gaming/how-indias-game-streaming-platform-is-growing-its-gaming-community)

### Inferences
- Supporting Free Fire MAX roughly doubles the addressable mobile battle-royale creator base. Free Fire's creators also skew younger and lower-income, so price sensitivity there is likely higher.
- Captioning must handle Hindi in Devanagari and in romanized Hinglish, plus code-switched speech. Accurate Hinglish speech-to-text and gaming slang ("chicken dinner", "full squad wipe", "1v4 clutch") are likely key differentiators. This is an inference; no sourced statistic was found.

### Gaps
