# YouTube native creator tools (2025 to Nov 2026) vs Kaatclip

Researched 12 Nov 2026. Baseline already in `reports/Competitor feature audit and creator gaps.md` (5 Nov 2026): Studio AI clip suggestions live in India and Hindi; live highlight Shorts need vertical mobile-app streams and exclude screencasts. Items below marked **NEW** are not in that audit.

Markings: **REPLACES** = YouTube now does something Kaatclip does; **GAP** = YouTube leaves a hole Kaatclip fills; **OPPORTUNITY** = Kaatclip could build on top of it.

## 1. Automatic highlights, clip suggestions and "make a Short from a stream/VOD"

### Takeaway
YouTube has three native clipping paths, and none fits a BGMI PC/OBS streamer well. Auto highlights after a live stream are mobile-app, vertical, non-screencast only. Studio's "Video Clips & Shorts" tool works on any long video or archived stream. Its AI suggestions (India, Hindi) are transcript-driven, it blurs the frame to fill 9:16 rather than building a layout, and it auto-links the source video. YouTube also killed the viewer Clip button (Apr 2026) and promised auto-suggested "clippable" moments, which I could not confirm have shipped. So a free YouTube rival is coming, but it is still generic.

### Cited Findings
- **Auto live highlights (GAP for OBS/PC):** After a *mobile* live stream, YouTube "can offer to save a Short highlight video that is automatically pulled from the stream"; it is saved private by default. Eligibility: "between 10 minutes and 12 hours", "must be in a vertical format and streamed using the YouTube mobile app", "cannot be a screencast", chat enabled, not Made for Kids. No countries or dates are listed. — [YouTube Help 2524542](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2524542?hl=en)
- AI highlights were announced at Made on YouTube on 16 Sep 2025: they "automatically select the best moments from a livestream" to turn into Shorts. That launch came with dual-format (horizontal + vertical at once) streaming. — [TechCrunch, 20 Sep 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/20/updates-to-studio-youtube-live-new-gen-ai-tools-and-everything-else-announced-at-made-on-youtube/); [Made On YouTube 2025 blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-2025/). Secondary explainers say it is "rolling out gradually" as Shorts drafts in Studio. None of them confirms encoder/desktop streams. — [search summary of livereacting / emarketer coverage](https://www.emarketer.com/content/youtube-expands-livestreaming-with-new-ads-ai-clips)
- **Studio "Video Clips & Shorts" tool (partial REPLACES):** It cuts segments from existing long videos into a Short or a video clip. You pick by transcript text or timeline. "AI suggestions (if available)" and an AI outline are offered in Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, **India**, Mexico, South Korea, Spain, UK, US, in English, French, German, **Hindi**, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish. Black bars show as "a blurred crop of the video" (a crude 9:16 fill, not a cam/gameplay layout). Autocaptions with editable colour, font and position. **The source video is automatically added as the Short's related video** (unless private, unlisted or Made for Kids). No launch date on the page. — [YouTube Help 15824265](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15824265?hl=en)
- **NEW, viewer Clip button removed (Apr 2026):** YouTube is discontinuing viewer-made Clips (moving viewers to "Share at Timestamp"). It keeps "Video Clips … in Studio today" so creators can "republish clips from longer videos and archived live streams". It said: "Later this year, we will be rolling out Video Clips to Shorts" plus auto-suggestions of "clippable" moments. — [Tubefilter, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/17/youtube-clipping-tool-viewer-clips-shorts/). Clips can't be made from streams without DVR or longer than 8 hours. — [YouTube Help 10332730 via search](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10332730?hl=en)
- Made on YouTube 2025 also listed "AI clip suggestions for video podcasts" for U.S. creators. — [TechCrunch, 20 Sep 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/20/updates-to-studio-youtube-live-new-gen-ai-tools-and-everything-else-announced-at-made-on-youtube/)
- **NEW, Made on YouTube 2026 (23 Sep 2026):** a Gemini Omni "conversational AI editing partner in Shorts" and in the YouTube Create app. It builds a first draft from selected clips and suggests trims of pauses, pacing fixes and on-screen text. No timing or country given. — [Google blog, 23 Sep 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-updates-2026/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-presents-new-ai-and-engagement-features-at-made-on-2026/831216). 9to5Google's and Google's recaps did not mention any live-to-Shorts auto clipping. — [9to5Google, 23 Sep 2026](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/23/made-on-youtube-2026/)
- "Edit with AI" (raw footage to first-draft video) was announced 16 Sep 2025 without dates or countries. — [Made On YouTube 2025](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-2025/)

### Inferences
- For a BGMI streamer on OBS, the *automatic* path (post-live highlight) does not apply. The *manual-plus-AI* path (Studio Video Clips on the archived stream) does apply, in India, in Hindi. It cannot see HUD kill events (it uses the transcript), has no ranking across a 4-6 hour stream, no cam+gameplay split layout, no zoom, no hook band, no scheduling queue and no Hinglish-specific handling. Kaatclip's edge is game events, layouts and the batch/schedule workflow, not "AI finds clips".
- The Studio tool auto-sets the source as the related video. That is the one thing Kaatclip *cannot* do through the API (see Q3), so YouTube's own tool has a structural funnel advantage. Kaatclip's stream link in the description is the workaround.
- Watch item: when "Video Clips to Shorts" plus auto "clippable" suggestions ship (promised for 2026), check whether they cover archived encoder streams. That is the threat that would turn REPLACES from partial to strong for casual users.
- Removing the viewer Clip button removes a free fan-clipping channel. That is an OPPORTUNITY for Kaatclip's "!clip" chat markers as a replacement for streamers whose fans used to clip.

### Gaps
- Could not find an official launch date for Studio AI clip suggestions, nor confirm whether post-live AI highlights now include encoder streams in any country (Dexerto's Sep 2025 article returned HTTP 525).
- Could not confirm whether "Video Clips to Shorts" or auto "clippable moment" suggestions shipped by Nov 2026; the October 2026 roundup does not mention it ([air.io Oct 2026](https://air.io/en/trending/youtube-updates-october-2026)).
- Quality: no independent test of Studio AI suggestions on gameplay with little speech. The owner should run one BGMI VOD through it (already a to-do in the audit).

## 2. Shorts features 2025-2026 (length, AI tools, dubbing, A/B tests, thumbnails, series, collabs, remix, monetisation, originality)

### Takeaway
The biggest 2026 change for Kaatclip is not a creation tool but a ranking one. Since 1 Oct 2026, Shorts recommendations cut reach for re-uploads and "template-based bulk changes". Elsewhere, A/B testing still excludes Shorts (video-cut testing announced, Shorts unclear), custom Shorts thumbnails arrived (YPP first, desktop) and became API-settable on 3 Nov 2026, Shorts can run 3 minutes, and auto-dubbing covers Hindi.

### Cited Findings
- **NEW, Shorts originality ranking (1 Oct 2026):** Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie (Creator Insider) said remixed clips must add value, "not just VO descriptions of what's happening on screen, minor technical edits, or template-based bulk changes". Creators should bring "their own commentary, analysis, and storytelling". Channels that shift to original content get reach re-evaluated. The video "doesn't discuss creators cutting Shorts from their own long-form videos". No start date was given. — [Search Engine Journal, 1 Oct 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/); also [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-cracks-down-on-clipping-channels-in-shorts-update-prioritizing-original-content-3415700/), [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-updates-shorts-algorithm-to-put-more-focus-on-original-content/831976). It affects reach, not monetisation (that stays under the reused-content policy). — [search summary of ppc.land / SMT](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)
- **Inauthentic content policy (15 Jul 2025):** YPP renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" to target mass-produced and repetitive videos. YouTube called it a clarification, "not a new policy". The reused-content rules were unchanged: reused material is fine with "significant original commentary, modifications, or educational or entertainment value". — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Social Samosa (India)](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- **Shorts length:** up to 3 minutes for square/vertical videos (since Oct 2024; still the rule in 2026). Third-party sources only. One claims it is limited to select creators; others say it is universal. Audio Library tracks may cap at ~90 s. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be/); [Async](https://async.com/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be/)
- **Test & Compare:** thumbnails (2024), then titles from Dec 2025, up to 3 variants, judged on watch time, desktop Studio. **Not available for Shorts** per the YouTube Help page and vidIQ. — [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-launches-new-title-testing-tool); sources disagree on whether access is YPP-gated or all eligible creators ([search summary](https://thumbnailtest.com/news/youtube-test-and-compare/))
- **NEW, video-cut A/B testing (announced 23-25 Sep 2026):** creators upload up to three cuts and get retention curves per cut, "coming soon". Shorts coverage is ambiguous: the narration says "shorts or long form", and the description says "Upload different video cuts for Shorts to test hooks". — [PPC Land, 27 Sep 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-to-test-up-to-3-versions-of-one-video-on-real-viewers-shorts-too/). Also "multiple thumbnails: up to three per video, YouTube shows the most relevant" — "soon". — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-presents-new-ai-and-engagement-features-at-made-on-2026/831216)
- **NEW, custom Shorts thumbnails (25 Jul 2026):** YPP channels first, upload desktop-only (frame pick stays on mobile), three suggested thumbnails on desktop, no A/B testing. — [PPC Land, 26 Jul 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/). API support followed on 3 Nov 2026 (see Q3).
- **NEW, Shorts series (23 Sep 2026):** seasons and episodes, custom thumbnails, sequence playback, rolling out from 23 Sep 2026. Members-only episodes "in the coming weeks" (Oct 2026). — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/23/made-on-youtube-2026/); [air.io Oct 2026](https://air.io/en/trending/youtube-updates-october-2026)
- **AI generation:** Veo 3 Fast in Shorts (free; motion transfer, restyle, add props), "Speech to song" (Lyria 2), "Edit with AI". All announced 16 Sep 2025, no country dates. — [Made On YouTube 2025](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-2025/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/20/updates-to-studio-youtube-live-new-gen-ai-tools-and-everything-else-announced-at-made-on-youtube/)
- **Auto-dubbing:** expanded on ~5 Feb 2026 to 27 languages, Hindi included; Expressive Speech in 8 languages; creators can upload their own tracks or turn it off. Lip sync remains a limited pilot. — [India TV News, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/youtube-auto-dubbing-feature-expands-to-all-users-now-supports-27-languages-2026-02-05-1029070); [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/unlocking-a-global-audience-with-auto-dubbing/)
- **Collaborations:** up to five co-creators credited on one video (Shorts included), announced Sep 2025, testing from Aug 2025. Cap reported as 4 or 5. No India-specific rollout found. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/20/updates-to-studio-youtube-live-new-gen-ai-tools-and-everything-else-announced-at-made-on-youtube/); [Business Today, 4 Aug 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/youtube-starts-testing-new-creator-collaboration-tools-like-instagram-collaboration-tool-487716-2025-08-04)
- **Remix:** the Remix button moved into the Share menu (May 2026). — [YouTube Help, Creator updates 9072033](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9072033). An AI remix tool launched in March 2026 lets creators reimagine others' clips; how it interacts with the originality update is unclear. — [search summary, aiweekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/youtube-downranks-re-uploaded-shorts-ai-remix-status-unclear)
- **Monetisation:** Monthly Shorts Bonus pilot from 15 Oct 2026, invite-only, US/UK/DE/BR/JP/KR (**not India**). A $20 payout-threshold test from 26 Oct 2026 includes **India**. — [air.io Oct 2026](https://air.io/en/trending/youtube-updates-october-2026)
- **Studio:** Ask Studio (AI channel insights; Sep 2025), Inspiration tab updates, likeness detection open beta for YPP (Sep 2025), and speaking-voice detection in test (Sep 2026). — [Made On YouTube 2025](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-2025/); [Social Media Today 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-presents-new-ai-and-engagement-features-at-made-on-2026/831216)

### Inferences
- **Originality ranking cuts both ways.** It is a threat to Kaatclip if the output looks like "template-based bulk changes" (the same hook band, layout and caption style stamped on every clip). It is an OPPORTUNITY to position Kaatclip as making a creator's *own* clips with their own voice and commentary. Concretely: keep the creator's mic and commentary in, vary hooks, let the creator write or approve hook text, and avoid one fixed template across every post. The originality guidance does not address creators clipping their own streams, so the own-content case is probably safe but unconfirmed.
- **A/B testing is still a GAP for Shorts** (title and thumbnail tests exclude Shorts). If video-cut tests ship for Shorts, Kaatclip can supply the 2-3 alternative cuts or hooks automatically: an OPPORTUNITY rather than a build-your-own A/B engine (the audit already keeps "own A/B tests" skipped).
- **Thumbnails:** a Shorts cover picker in Kaatclip is now worthwhile, since custom Shorts thumbnails exist and are API-settable.
- **Dubbing / Veo / speech-to-song / Gemini editing assistant REPLACE** anything generic Kaatclip might add (dubbing, B-roll generation, generic trim suggestions). Kaatclip should keep skipping these.
- **Shorts series** is an OPPORTUNITY: auto-group daily BGMI clips into a "season" (for example a tournament week), if series become API-addressable (not found in the API changelog).
- 3-minute Shorts make Kaatclip's longer presets (60-180 s clutch rounds) valid Shorts.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube page found confirming India availability of Veo 3 in Shorts, Edit with AI, the Gemini editing assistant, Collaborations or Shorts series.
- Hype (fan-boosting for channels under 500k subs) and "Hype with Jewels" (reported live in four countries) were not researched in depth. India status unknown.
- Inspiration tab changes in 2026: no detail found.

## 3. Data API / Analytics API changes (2025 to Nov 2026) and policy for uploads

### Takeaway
Several API changes in 2025-2026 directly help Kaatclip. Upload quota cost fell from ~1,600 to ~100 units (Dec 2025). `videos.insert` got its own quota bucket (Jun 2026). Unverified projects' uploads are no longer forced private (2 Nov 2026). `thumbnails.set` works for Shorts (3 Nov 2026). Analytics can now separate the Shorts feed and vertical live feed as playback locations (6 Nov 2026). There is still **no API to set a Short's related video**, and nothing new on captions or scheduling.

### Cited Findings (Data API v3, [revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history))
- **26 Mar 2025:** from 31 Mar 2025, Shorts `viewCount` counts every start or replay with no minimum watch time.
- **10 Jul 2025:** from 21 Jul 2025, `videos.list` `chart=mostPopular` draws from the Trending Music, Movies and Gaming charts (the Trending page was deprecated).
- **4 Dec 2025:** documented upload quota cost dropped from ~1,600 to ~100 units.
- **1 Jun 2026:** granular quota buckets. `videos.insert` and `search.list` each get their own bucket; per-method increases can be requested.
- **3 Jun 2026:** new `videos.batchGetStats` (1 unit, own bucket, 10,000 units/day default).
- **7 Jul 2026:** new `brandPartner` part on videos (`channelId`/`channelHandle`) settable via `videos.insert`/`update`, for brand-partner links.
- **27 Aug 2026:** public view counting is aligned across long-form, Live and Shorts (counted from the first frame). Monetisation uses "qualified Shorts views" and "qualified watch hours".
- **1 Sep 2026:** `videos.getRating` accepts the `youtube.readonly` scope.
- **14 Sep 2026:** `thumbnails.set` max size rises from 2 MB to 50 MB.
- **30 Sep 2026:** comments get `snippet.imageUrl` (image/GIF comments; rolls out Nov 2026).
- **2 Nov 2026:** uploads from **unverified** API projects are **no longer forced to private** (reverses the July 2020 rule). An omitted `privacyStatus` defaults to public.
- **3 Nov 2026:** `thumbnails.set` can set custom thumbnails on **Shorts** after processing; the `thumbnails` resource adds `shortsAspectRatio`.
- No 2025-2026 entries cover captions, related-video linking, or `containsSyntheticMedia` (the AI-disclosure field dates from 30 Oct 2024).

### Cited Findings (Analytics / Reporting API, [revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/revision_history))
- **26 Mar / 24 Apr / 24 Jun 2025:** `views` for Shorts count every start or replay. The new **`engagedViews`** metric keeps the old method (Targeted Queries from 30 Apr 2025, Bulk Reports from 30 Jun 2025 with new report versions).
- **15 Jan 2026:** Reach reports for channels (`channel_reach_basic_a1`, `channel_reach_combined_a1`) with `video_thumbnail_impressions` and `video_thumbnail_impressions_ctr`.
- **9 Mar 2026:** `ageGroup` includes users YouTube estimates are under 18.
- **27 Aug 2026:** public `views` count from the first frame (autoplay and hover too). `engagedViews` is unchanged and still drives CTR, average view duration and monetisation.
- **9 Sep 2026:** docs state a 48-72 h processing delay and recommend `videos.list` for real-time stats. Monetary metrics need the monetary scope plus YPP (403 otherwise).
- **6 Nov 2026:** new playback locations: Analytics API `SHORTS_FEED` and `IMMERSIVE_LIVE_FEED`; Reporting API values 11 (vertical live feed) and 12 (Music Samples tab).
- `creatorContentType` is unchanged (introduced 15 Dec 2022).

### Inferences
- **OPPORTUNITY / unblock:** with unverified uploads no longer forced private (2 Nov 2026), Kaatclip in Testing mode can actually publish or schedule public Shorts before Google's verification completes. The audit's verification and audit-form steps are still needed for user caps and quota, but the "everything private until audited" blocker is gone. Kaatclip should still send an explicit `privacyStatus` (audit row 8), because omitting it now means public.
- The upload quota (own bucket, ~100 units per upload) roughly removes quota as a near-term limit for a few dozen creators. The audit's "~100 posted clips/day" figure should be rechecked against the new per-method buckets.
- `thumbnails.set` for Shorts (3 Nov 2026) makes the audit's planned Shorts cover picker buildable now (YPP gating on the UI side may still apply to custom Shorts thumbnails; the API docs entry did not say).
- Analytics: use `engagedViews` (not `views`) when ranking clips by content type, since public `views` changed twice (Mar 2025, Aug 2026). The new `SHORTS_FEED` / `IMMERSIVE_LIVE_FEED` playback locations let "stats by content type" show how much Shorts-feed and vertical-live-feed traffic feeds the channel: an OPPORTUNITY for Kaatclip's teaser-Short and stream-link features to show measured impact.
- **GAP stays:** no API field for a Short's related video, so the in-player link to the stream can only be set by hand in the YouTube app/Studio, or via YouTube's own clip tool, which sets it automatically. Kaatclip can remind creators to set it (deep link to Studio) and put the stream link in the description.

### Gaps
- No API changelog entry about AI-generated-content disclosure changes, Shorts series, collaborations or Test & Compare via API in 2025-2026 (none appears; the API page was only read to 100k of 223k characters, but entries are newest-first, so 2025-2026 is covered).
- Exact per-bucket default quota for `videos.insert` after 1 Jun 2026 was not stated in the summary read.

## 4. Live features (chat tools, polls, goals, co-streaming, vertical live, viewer clipping)

### Takeaway
YouTube Live gained a lot in 2025-2026: dual-format streaming, a vertical live feed, Practice Mode, Playables in vertical streams, side-by-side ads, minigames, reaction streams, Watch With, and announced 2027 items (live showdowns, matchmaking, real-time auto-dubbing). It removed viewer Clips. None of these produce edited, ranked Shorts from a PC/OBS stream, so they are mostly neutral to Kaatclip. Dual-format and the vertical live feed make vertical *during* the stream more native, a mild REPLACES for "teaser" content.

### Cited Findings
- Made on YouTube 2025 (16 Sep 2025), "our largest upgrade to live ever": dual-format horizontal+vertical streaming, AI highlights to Shorts, live reactions to other broadcasts, minigames for viewers, "side-by-side" ads. Over 30% of daily logged-in viewers watched live content in Q2 2025. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/20/updates-to-studio-youtube-live-new-gen-ai-tools-and-everything-else-announced-at-made-on-youtube/); [Made On YouTube 2025](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-2025/)
- 2026 Creator updates: Live Practice Mode rolling out to all creators on mobile. Playables as mini-activities on mobile **vertical** live streams (Mar 2026, "certain countries"). Up to three channel guidelines shown in comments and live chat (Apr 2026, global). — [YouTube Help, Creator updates](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9072033)
- Made on YouTube 2026 (23 Sep 2026): live showdowns (fan-funded competitions; 2027), creator matchmaking (early 2027), Watch With expanding to all eligible videos (later 2026), real-time live auto-dubbing pilot in English and Spanish (early 2027), AI comment moderation test. More than 600M logged-in viewers watched live daily in Aug 2026. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-presents-new-ai-and-engagement-features-at-made-on-2026/831216); [search summary of 2026 coverage](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/23/made-on-youtube-2026/)
- **Viewer clipping:** viewer Clips discontinued from Apr 2026 in favour of Share at Timestamp; creators keep Studio Video Clips from archived streams. — [Tubefilter, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/17/youtube-clipping-tool-viewer-clips-shorts/)
- **Analytics:** a vertical live feed exists as a playback location (`IMMERSIVE_LIVE_FEED`, 6 Nov 2026). — [Analytics revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/revision_history)
- Live chat API: `messageDeletedEvent`/`messageRetractedEvent` were removed from `liveChatMessages` docs on 23 Jun 2026 because the API doesn't return them. No new chat-reading features were found. — [Data API revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history)

### Inferences
- **GAP Kaatclip fills:** with viewer Clips gone, nothing native lets the audience flag moments in a live stream. Kaatclip's "!clip" chat markers become the only fan-clipping path for YouTube streamers. That is worth stating in marketing (dated: Apr 2026).
- Dual-format streaming plus the vertical live feed mean OBS streamers can push a vertical version live. If a streamer does that from mobile vertical, YouTube's auto highlights might apply. A PC/OBS dual-format stream is still not a "mobile app" stream per the Help page, so highlights should not apply (unconfirmed).
- Live auto-dubbing (2027) and showdowns are irrelevant to clipping. Showdowns or matchmaking could create collab moments worth clipping (OPPORTUNITY: a "collab" clip type with Collaborations credit, set manually).

### Gaps
- Polls, Super Chat goals, membership changes, Hype and "Go Live Together" in 2025-2026 were not researched (out of budget); no India-specific live announcements were found.
- Whether dual-format streams from OBS (via YouTube's vertical ingest) get post-live AI highlights: no source found.
- Whether the 2025 AI highlights reached India: no source found.
