# What Indian gaming streamers struggle with and ask for (2025-2026), checked against Kaatclip

Scope note: these notes report what is NEW compared with the two earlier reports (`reports/Gaming YouTuber content workflow needs.md`, `reports/BGMI AI clipper market validation.md`). Points already covered there (EY 70%+ brand-deal share, ₹20-40 RPM, RMG ban, AIR Shorts-share study, intern/freelancer editor rates, YouTube free tools, Kofluence ₹35K-90K campaign range, editor/agency buyers) are only referenced, not repeated in detail.

Evidence-strength labels used below: **Survey** (sample-based report), **Platform/official** (company announcement), **Press** (trade journalism, often based on one or two interviews), **Anecdote** (one named creator), **Vendor** (seller's own blog/estimate).

Honest overall caveat: India-specific *gaming-creator* voices about their workflow are thin on the open web. Searches of Reddit (r/IndianGaming, r/NewTubers etc.) returned no India gaming-streamer threads on tooling; the strongest creator quotes come from trade press interviews. Most numbers below are general Indian creator-economy numbers, not gaming-only.

## 1. How Indian gaming streamers grow and earn now, and what sponsor admin they do by hand

### Takeaway
Income is a stack: brand deals (the largest), Super Chat/memberships and now YouTube Gifts on vertical lives, Rooter virtual gifts, affiliate links, Krafton creator competitions, and merchandise/events at the top. The admin around brand money is painful and manual: chasing late payments for months, informal WhatsApp/DM deals with no payment terms, barter offers, and weak GST/invoicing, with only ~15% of creators registered as a business.

- The HashFame "brands can't reach me / verify me" finding strengthens the earlier report's auto media kit idea: a verified, shareable stats page with a contact form. Kaatclip already pulls channel stats by Shorts/live/videos, so this is a small step. Kaatclip: **partly covered** (stats exist, no shareable kit).

Kaatclip's transcript makes brand-mention detection cheap. Kaatclip: **not covered**. Evidence: inferred from brand-side accountability stats, no creator asked for it by name.
- YouTube Gifts only on vertical lives, and YouTube's AI highlights also tied to vertical lives, keep pushing creators toward vertical streaming. If BGMI PC/OBS streamers add a vertical second output, Kaatclip's "horizontal streamer" edge narrows. Worth watching.
- KRAFTON's creator competitions (Survivor League, Mahayuddh) mean creators need fast clips during short event windows and posts pointing fans to "join my team". An "event mode" (campaign hashtag, publisher-required tags, daily recap Short) is a plausible feature. Kaatclip: **not covered**. Evidence: inferred.

## 2. Instagram Reels vs YouTube Shorts, and other platforms (Moj, Josh, ShareChat, X, Facebook Gaming)

### Takeaway
Instagram dominates Indian brand spend overall (93% of brands prioritise it), while YouTube is where gaming streamers earn from lives and long-form. Gaming creators interviewed say they use both: short clips on Instagram and Shorts for reach, YouTube lives/long videos for depth and money. For a sponsor-funded gaming creator, missing Instagram means missing where brands look first.

### Cited Findings
- Kofluence 2026: **93.1% of brands prioritise Instagram** as their main influencer channel; Instagram is the primary platform for 3.3-3.7M of India's ~4-4.4M active creators. No YouTube share given; not gaming-specific. Survey/platform data — [MediaNews4U](https://www.medianews4u.com/15-of-indias-creators-are-now-registered-business-entities-as-influencer-marketing-crosses-a-structural-threshold-kofluence/); [Editorji](https://www.editorji.com/business-news/kofluence-reveals-indias-creator-economy-insights-1778734222031)
- Trade press: Instagram "remains the most effective platform" for gaming brand collaborations, with YouTube Shorts "showing promising results"; Reels bonuses are invite-only and inconsistent. Press (search summary) — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/game-stream-earn-how-much-do-top-gamers-make-144867.html) (page returned 403 on fetch; claim not verified directly)
- Mukul Anchal (8Bit Mafia, S8UL-adjacent creator): short clips act as hooks that lead new viewers to longer content; "YouTube supports deep engagement and high retention, while Instagram and YouTube Shorts help with reach"; long-form monetises well because viewers spend hours. Press interview, June 2025 — [Social Ketchup](https://www.socialketchup.in/ketchup-talks-interviews/popularity-of-long-form-gaming-content-9314707)

## 3. Editors, language, thumbnails, community, stream schedules and notifying fans

### Takeaway
Creators who grow hire help (editors/teams) and rising production expectations push costs up; regional languages are a clear growth vector with brands asking for vernacular briefs; Discord is the off-stream community hub cited by gaming creators, with WhatsApp groups for everyday chat. Direct evidence on thumbnails and schedule notifications from Indian gaming creators was not found.

## 4. Industry reports with numbers (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
The best 2025-26 numbers are general Indian creator-economy figures (Kofluence 2026, EY, BCG, ISB-Hashfame, HashFame); Lumikai's latest report (March 2026, covering CY25) frames a "player to creator" shift and a smaller gamer base after the RMG ban. No report breaks out gaming creators' income sources or tool spend.

### Cited Findings
- Lumikai State of India Interactive Media 2025 (5th edition, released March 2026): India video games market ex-RMG $1.5B, +17% YoY in CY25; gamer base fell 609M to 555M (-9%) after the RMG ban; founder Salone Sehgal calls the player-to-creator shift one of its most defining conclusions. Report via press — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/lumikai-india-gaming-market-1-5-billion-rmg-ban-2025); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/05/india-shifts-gears-interactive-media-digital-tool-lumikai)

- EY: India's creator economy ~₹125B in 2024, projected ~₹500B by 2030 (25% CAGR); influencer marketing ₹3,375 cr, projected ₹3,975 cr by 2026. Report via press — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/indias-creator-economy-set-to-touch-rs-500-billion-by-2030-ey-143175.html)

### Inferences
Suggested ranking of NEW feature candidates (by evidence strength x cost for Kaatclip):
1. **Instagram Reels-ready export** (file + caption + hashtags, later API) — strongest evidence (brand-side survey), low cost for export-only.
2. **Shareable media kit + "work with me" page** from existing stats — HashFame lost-deals survey + brand accountability; low cost.
3. **Sponsor deal tracker** (deliverables, due dates, invoice/GST fields, paid/unpaid with reminders via existing Telegram) — strong anecdotal + press evidence of late payment pain; medium cost; no gaming creator asked for it by name.
4. **Sponsor proof clips/report** (detect brand mentions in transcript, cut them, add views) — inferred from brand accountability; low-medium cost using existing transcript.
5. **Discord webhook posting** of new Shorts and "live now/teaser" alerts — creator interviews; very low cost.
6. **Affiliate/brand link field per Short** (description + Related link) — new YouTube India features; very low cost.
7. **Regional captions** (Telugu, Tamil, Bengali) — survey-level vernacular demand; medium cost.
8. **Away/break mode** that keeps a backlog of approved Shorts scheduled — anecdotal burnout evidence; low cost on top of calendar.
9. **Publisher event mode** (campaign tags, daily recap Shorts during KRAFTON creator leagues) — inferred; low cost.

### Gaps
- No direct Indian BGMI streamer (mid-tier, 10K-500K) voice found describing their clipping/posting workflow in 2025-26; the earlier report's r/Twitch evidence remains the best workflow evidence, and it is not Indian.
- Willingness to pay for sponsor/admin tools specifically is unknown.
- Instagram vs YouTube effect on gaming sponsorship rates for mid-tier BGMI creators is unmeasured; only top-creator estimates exist.
