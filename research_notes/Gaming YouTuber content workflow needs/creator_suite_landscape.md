# Creator content-management suites and tools beyond AI clippers (landscape for GCM Clipper, as of 4 Nov 2026)

Scope note: OpusClip, Vizard, Klap, Reap and Eklipse posting/pricing, and Buffer/Metricool, are already covered in `reports/AI clipper autoposting and multigame gaps.md`. Only new material on those is added here. Prices are USD from the pages read on 4 Nov 2026 unless stated otherwise. Rupee figures marked "≈" use an assumed ₹88 = $1, which does not come from any source. Several vendor pages could not be read (Streamlabs /pricing returned empty; no official Spikes Studio, Medal, Captions or Canva pricing page was read). Where only third-party or competitor pages exist, this is flagged.

## 1. YouTube Studio itself (2025–2026): what YouTube already gives for free

### Takeaway
YouTube now covers several jobs for free: AI highlight Shorts after a stream (mobile-first, rollout unclear), clipping from archived streams in Studio, A/B tests of up to 3 titles and thumbnails, auto-dubbing that includes Hindi, Tamil and Telugu, an AI chat assistant (Ask Studio), a Hindi-voiceover "Edit with AI" in YouTube Create, and dual-format live. GCM should not rebuild dubbing, A/B testing or generic idea generation. It should use them through links or the API, and compete on game-aware moment picking from long desktop and PC streams, Hinglish captions, and an approve-then-post queue. YouTube does not offer the last two.

### Cited Findings
**Live highlights / auto Shorts from streams**
- Announced at Made on YouTube, 16 Sep 2025: "AI-powered highlights (Live recap)". After a stream, AI picks the most compelling moments and makes ready-to-share Shorts. No rollout date is given. The post points to a community post for availability. — [YouTube Blog, live updates](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/live-updates/)
- The same announcement covers: Practice mode; horizontal and vertical streams at the same time with one shared chat ("soon", no date); React Live; side-by-side ads; switching from public to members-only; Playables on Live (75+ titles). — [YouTube Blog, live updates](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/live-updates/)
- A third-party 2026 guide says the highlights are saved as **Private drafts** in Studio. It lists eligibility as a stream of 10 min–12 h, **vertical, from the mobile app**, with live chat on. **Not verified on an official YouTube page.** — [SocialBee, Oct 2026 YouTube updates](https://socialbee.com/blog/youtube-updates/); also [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-launches-ai-powered-highlight-tool-to-automatically-create-stream-clips-3252018); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-reply-filters-live-stream-highlights-shorts/760959/)
- A third-party video summary says the AI highlight feature is "currently exclusive to mobile live streams". This is a single secondary source. — [Recapio digest of Trevor Baker video](https://recapio.com/digest/youtube-s-new-vertical-horizontal-livestreaming-tool-what-you-need-to-know-by-trevor-baker)
- **April 2026:** YouTube is retiring viewer-made Clips. "Video Clips is available in Studio today": creators can republish clips cut from longer videos **and archived live streams**. Video Clips for Shorts and "auto-suggestions" of the most "clippable" moments are promised "later this year" (2026), with no date. — [Tubefilter, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/17/youtube-clipping-tool-viewer-clips-shorts/amp/)
- YouTube has separately tested a long-form "video highlights" tool that turns engaging segments into Shorts, on a small group of desktop Studio users. — [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/youtube-new-video-highlights-tool); [Tubefilter, Jan 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/01/08/youtube-long-form-content-clip-shorts-tool/amp/)

**Titles, thumbnails, ideas**
- Test & Compare now handles **up to three titles and thumbnails per video**, building on thumbnail tests that have been used over 15M times since 2023. — [YouTube Blog, Made on YouTube 2025 Studio post](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/)
- A third-party guide says the winner is picked by watch time per impression, not click-through rate. Not verified officially. — [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtubes-new-title-ab-testing-tool-everything-creators-need-to-know)
- **Ask Studio**: an AI chat for analytics, community feedback and ideas ("catch me up on how my last video is performing"). — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/)
- The **Inspiration tab** (AI ideas, titles, thumbnails, outlines) is being deprecated: "Starting August 2026, we are gradually deprecating the Inspiration tab". Ask Studio replaces it. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15575509?hl=en)
- Collaborations: up to 5 co-creators per video. Likeness detection is in open beta for all YPP creators. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/)

**Dubbing and Indian languages**
- Auto-dubbing supported 20 languages in Sep 2025, and lip-sync testing was due "in the coming months". — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-made-on-youtube-2025/)
- The official help page lists Hindi, Tamil and Telugu among source languages and as English-to-X targets. Hindi and Telugu are marked for "expressive speech", which copies pitch and intonation. — [YouTube Help: Use automatic dubbing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15569972?hl=en)
- Feb 2026 press: auto-dubbing opened to all users and grew to 27 languages. Expressive speech is rolling out to all channels for 8 languages, including Hindi. — [India TV News, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/youtube-auto-dubbing-feature-expands-to-all-users-now-supports-27-languages-2026-02-05-1029070); [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/?p=332404)
- YouTube's Hindi auto-captions exist but have accuracy problems. A third-party guide says they are "riddled with errors" when speakers mix Hindi and English. — [inkl / The Hindu](https://www.inkl.com/news/13-years-later-hindi-auto-captioning-launched-for-youtube); [you-tldr guide](https://you-tldr.com/blog/youtube-transcript-in-hindi)

**YouTube Create (India-launched mobile editor)**
- It launched first in India as an Android beta. A Business Standard piece (Dec 2025 by URL) says it now runs on Android and iOS in India. — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/youtube-create-for-ios-ai-video-creation-tools-iphone-features-125121600807_1.html); [GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=1189374)
- At launch, auto-captions came in English, Hindi and Spanish, along with background noise removal and speed tools. — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/apps/youtube-create-lets-you-easily-edit-videos-on-mobile-know-more-69045.html); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/youtube-create-app-access-and-steps-to-use/470681)
- **"Edit with AI"** (16 Sep 2025, Google India blog) turns camera-roll footage into a first draft: it picks the best moments and adds music, transitions and a voiceover **in English or Hindi** that reacts to the video. It is being tested on Shorts and in YouTube Create, expanding "in the coming weeks in India and select markets". Output carries SynthID labels. — [Google India Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/unpacking-the-magic-of-our-new-creative-tool-in-the-youtube-create-app/)

**Roles and approvals**
- Studio channel permissions use preset roles (e.g. Editor, Subtitle Editor, Viewer). No source showed a native "draft → approve → publish" step in YouTube. — [Social Media Today on channel permissions](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-updates-channel-permissions-controls-making-it-easier-to-assign-in/688578/); [Postly blog](https://blog.postly.ai/how-teams-should-approve-youtube-content/) (vendor blog)

### Inferences
- **Do not duplicate:** auto-dubbing (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu), title and thumbnail A/B tests, generic AI idea and title brainstorming, chapters, and plain clipping from your own archived streams. GCM can link to or call these instead. For example, GCM could suggest 2–3 Hinglish title variants and tell the creator to run them in Test & Compare, which saves building an A/B engine.
- **YouTube's native auto-highlights is the biggest direct threat**, but from the evidence it is mobile/vertical-stream-first, generic (no game-event awareness), and has no Hinglish caption styling. Most BGMI streamers go live from PC/OBS with phone capture, horizontal. GCM's pitch should be "game-aware picks from your horizontal stream, with Hinglish captions and facecam layout", and it should benchmark itself against YouTube's free highlights.
- The promised Studio "auto-suggestions of clippable moments" (late 2026) would reach long-form and archived streams. Watch for it: if it ships, generic "find highlights" becomes table stakes and free.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube Help page was found giving eligibility (desktop vs mobile, stream length, channel size) or GA status for live AI highlights as of Nov 2026.
- I did not verify whether Shorts "remix" or an AI Shorts editor now exists inside Studio mobile, or whether automatic chapters for live VODs have changed. No sources were checked.
- Comment-management features (the new reply filters were reported with live highlights in [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-reply-filters-live-stream-highlights-shorts/760959/)) were not read in detail.
- Whether YouTube Create got gaming-specific features (facecam layouts, kill zooms) is unknown; nothing found.

## 2. Streaming suites: Streamlabs, StreamElements, Restream, Medal, OBS plugins

### Takeaway
Streaming suites are moving into clipping. Streamlabs bundles Cross Clip (vertical reformat), "Replay" auto-highlights and an event-detecting "Sidekick" in Ultra. Restream auto-clips any recording over 10 minutes and posts to Shorts and Reels. These clippers are speech- or generic-based, English-first, and have no approval queue or Hinglish. Medal and StreamElements have no confirmed YouTube Shorts auto-posting. A gaming streamer already on Streamlabs Ultra (~$16–27/mo) gets "good enough" clipping in their existing bill. That is GCM's main pricing pressure.

### Cited Findings
- **Streamlabs Ultra** official page: "Starting at $15.75/mo" (billing basis not stated). Promotions: ULTRA10; STUDENT 50% off; SCREAMLABS ended 1 Nov 2026. Includes Multistream, full overlay library, 10 automations, **Sidekick (1,000 interactions/mo), described as an agent that detects in-game events and triggers automations**, "Producer Automations: your personal AI producer", Stream Shift, Collab Cam up to 11 guests, 10GB cloud, Xbox console streaming, Dual Output (horizontal and vertical layouts). — [Streamlabs Ultra](https://streamlabs.com/ultra)
- Ultra includes the Pro tiers of: **Cross Clip** (1-hour videos, 250GB; formats clips for TikTok, Shorts, Reels; free tier 30 min/15GB), **Talk Studio** (multistream, no watermark), **Podcast Editor** (40 h upload, no watermark), **Video Editor** (1080p60, no watermark). **Replay** (highlighter) "automatically pulls clips for social media". — [Streamlabs Ultra](https://streamlabs.com/ultra)
- Third-party reviews list Ultra at $27/mo or $189/yr and an "Ultra+" at $79/mo. Standalone prices conflict: Talk Studio $9/mo vs $4–12/mo; Cross Clip Pro $4.99/mo. **Not verified on an official page**; the official page shows "from $15.75/mo". — [Creator Stack Club](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/streamlabs); [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/streamlabs/pricing); [work-management.org](https://work-management.org/marketing/creators/streamlabs-review/)
- **Restream Clips**: the AI picks highlights, frames them and adds captions. It "relies on spoken content", so clear speech works best. Studio or encoder recordings over 10 min are clipped automatically ("Autopilot"). You can also clip uploads, public YouTube URLs, Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive. It handles videos over 3 h and downloads at 1080p. **Direct posting to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Rumble**, multiposting included. **No built-in scheduling of clips.** Translation into many languages; Hindi is not named in the list read. Plan limits are not given on the FAQ. — [Restream Clips FAQ](https://support.restream.io/en/articles/10559224-clips-general-faqs); [What are Restream Clips](https://restream.io/learn/clips/what-are-restream-clips/)
- **StreamElements SE.Live**: an OBS plugin, Windows-only (Mac support dropped). Multistreams to YouTube, TikTok, Kick, X and custom RTMP. No 2026 clip or Shorts feature found. — [SE.Live complete guide](https://support.streamelements.com/hc/en-us/articles/18771457842066-SE-Live-The-Complete-Guide-Setup-Features-Multistreaming-Troubleshooting)
- **Medal**: gaming-first clip capture and social feed with Discord sharing. A competitor (AutoClip) says it has no direct posting to Shorts, Reels or TikTok. Medal's own guide recommends share links for cross-posting. — [AutoClip vs Medal](https://autoclip.dev/compare/autoclip-vs-medal) (competitor); [Medal learn](https://medal.tv/learn/how-to-grow-gaming-content)
- Twitch has added automatic vertical clips, a Twitch-side precedent that may spread. — [Gaming Careers newsletter](https://gaming-careers.pages.dev/newsletters/twitch-launches-new-clip-features)

### Inferences
- Table stakes for streamer-facing tools in 2026: vertical reformatting, auto-captions, multistreaming, and posting to Shorts and Reels.
- Gaps across these suites: no approve-then-post queue with scheduling (Restream has none, Streamlabs is not documented), no Hindi or Hinglish captions documented, speech-led detection (Restream says so explicitly), and nothing for mobile battle-royale HUDs.
- Streamlabs' Sidekick "detects in-game events" is the closest suite-level threat to GCM's game-aware detection. Which games it covers and whether it feeds clip ranking was not established.
- Integration angle: GCM should accept OBS, Streamlabs and Restream recordings and YouTube VOD URLs as input, rather than compete on live production.

### Gaps
- Official Streamlabs annual and INR pricing could not be read (the pricing page fetched empty). Which games Sidekick supports, and whether Replay uses AI, are not stated.
- Restream Clips plan prices and quotas were not found. Kick-oriented tools were not researched.
- OBS plugin landscape (e.g. replay-buffer and vertical-canvas plugins) not researched.

## 3. Growth and analytics tools: vidIQ, TubeBuddy

### Takeaway
vidIQ and TubeBuddy own keyword research, competitor tracking, AI ideas and bulk metadata, at $3–49/mo. vidIQ's MAX tier now includes "up to 11 hours of short clipping and generations", so growth tools are moving into clipping too. Neither shows INR prices. Neither is game-aware or Hinglish-aware. GCM should not rebuild SEO research. A light "Hinglish title + tags suggestion" is enough.

### Cited Findings
- **vidIQ** (official pricing, read 4 Nov 2026): Free $0 (150 AI credits/mo; niche trends, ideas, AI Coach tips, **vidIQ MCP**). **Boost** $19/mo list or $199/yr (≈$16.58/mo; 2,000 credits; unlimited trend research, subscriber insights, personalised ideas, thumbnail tools, AI Coach "deep thinking"). **MAX** $49/mo list or $468/yr (≈$39/mo; 6,000 credits; 3x AI Coach, "Max Mode", **"up to 11 hours of short clipping and generations"**). **Enterprise** for brands, agencies and MCNs: **team roles and permissions**, cross-channel reporting. No INR amounts shown; "local pricing may vary". — [vidIQ pricing](https://vidiq.com/pricing/)
- **TubeBuddy** (official pricing): Pro $3/mo or $32/yr; Legend $15/mo or $144/yr; 3-year options $49/$199; Enterprise by contact. A/B testing of titles and thumbnails is Legend-only. Keyword Explorer on all plans. Competitor Scorecard is limited on Legend and full on Enterprise. AI Title Generator (Legend), Thumbnail Generator (all). Bulk find/replace and thumbnail overlays (Legend). "Suggested Shorts" on all plans. INR shown only as an estimate; billed in USD. — [TubeBuddy pricing](https://www.tubebuddy.com/pricing)
- Reviewers rate vidIQ stronger on AI ideas, and TubeBuddy cheaper with better bulk and A/B tools. TubeBuddy's AI suggestions are called "generic". — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/vidiq-vs-tubebuddy/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/vidiq-vs-tubebuddy/); [Lenspov](https://lenspov.com/articles/tubebuddy-vs-vidiq-2026)
- Third-party sites quote higher TubeBuddy prices (Pro $12/mo, Legend ~$26–27/mo), which conflicts with the official page above. **The official figures are used here.** — [Thumbnail Test](https://thumbnailtest.com/guides/vidiq-vs-tubebuddy/)

### Inferences
- Since YouTube's own Test & Compare is free, TubeBuddy's paid A/B testing has lost much of its value. GCM should not build A/B testing.
- vidIQ adding clipping to MAX ($39–49/mo) plus Enterprise team roles shows convergence: growth tools are becoming "creator OS" suites. GCM's defensible edge stays game events plus Hinglish plus an India price.
- vidIQ ships an MCP server (Free plan). A future GCM integration could pull keyword and trend ideas from it instead of building SEO.

### Gaps
- No official INR pricing for either tool. Neither page lists gaming-specific features (e.g. game-tag trends).
- Whether vidIQ's clipping is game-aware or supports Hindi was not checked.

## 4. Editors and design tools used in India

### Takeaway
Indian gaming creators edit mostly on free mobile apps (VN, KineMaster, InShot) and desktop free tools (DaVinci Resolve, Clipchamp), plus YouTube Create, which has Hindi captions and an AI first-draft. CapCut appears blocked in India (HTTP 451). No source documented gaming-specific features such as kill-zoom, facecam split layouts or BGMI templates in these mainstream editors. Caption tools such as Submagic support Hindi but not documented Hinglish (Roman script). This leaves a gaming-specific, Hinglish caption-and-layout editor open.

### Cited Findings
- GamingNation (Indian gaming site, updated Jul 2026) recommends **VN** as the CapCut replacement: free core, paid VN Pro, ads, multi-track, **speed ramps for kill highlights**, keyframes, LUTs, captions. Also Shotcut, Kdenlive, OpenShot, Clipchamp (free, 1080p cap) and DaVinci Resolve 21. It cites **CapCut as unavailable in India**: Google Play India 404, capcut.com HTTP 451 "Unavailable For Legal Reasons". No facecam, zoom templates or Hindi features are listed for any of them. — [GamingNation](https://gamingnation.in/best-free-video-editing-software-gaming/)
- Other 2026 roundups name KineMaster as an established Indian mobile editor and InShot as a light option for gaming Shorts. These are SEO-quality sources. One lists CapCut as still popular, which conflicts with the above. — [Inventiva](https://www.inventiva.co.in/?p=325321); [blogspot roundup](https://kidsfunzone2026.blogspot.com/2026/05/top-ai-video-editing-apps-for-gaming-youtubers-2026.html)
- **YouTube Create**: free, Hindi auto-captions, and Hindi-voiceover AI first-draft (see section 1). — [Google India Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/unpacking-the-magic-of-our-new-creative-tool-in-the-youtube-create-app/)
- **Canva Pro India**: one tracker says ₹4,500/yr, "verified at canva.com/en_in/pricing in May 2026". Others say ₹3,999/yr or ₹500/mo. Price-rise reports conflict on whether India was included. **No official page read.** — [productgrowth.in](https://productgrowth.in/tools/design/canva/); [aitoolsdose](https://aitoolsdose.pages.dev/canva-ai-review-india); [Dhanam](https://english.dhanamonline.com/news/canva-hikes-global-subscription-prices-by-300-what-does-it-mean-for-indian-users-6949199); [Bloomberg on cheap emerging-market plans](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-14/canva-tests-cheap-plans-in-emerging-markets-after-india-progress)
- **Submagic**: Hindi is listed among about 48 caption languages, per competitor pages. Hinglish (Roman) is not confirmed. Prices conflict ($12–69/member/mo; $19–20 starter). Pro has custom caption templates. No gaming templates found. — [videocaptions.ai](https://www.videocaptions.ai/alternatives/submagic); [subscribed.fyi](https://subscribed.fyi/submagic); [Submagic Hindi blog](https://www.submagic.co/hi/blog/reasons-to-use-submagic-for-ai-captions)

### Inferences
- Table stakes for an editor: trim, captions, speed ramps, text overlays and export for Shorts and Reels, all available free in VN and YouTube Create. GCM's editor should be a **thin review editor** (trim, caption fix, layout pick), not a full NLE.
- Differentiators few mainstream editors document: preset **facecam-over-gameplay vertical layouts**, **auto-zoom on kill or knock events**, **Roman-Hinglish caption styles** in gaming fonts, BGMI-safe templates (no other-game promotion). These are where GCM can be "the gaming editor".
- CapCut's absence in India removes the strongest free template editor, so there is room for an India-hosted alternative.

### Gaps
- Official pages for VN, InShot, KineMaster, Adobe Express, Descript and the Captions app were not read. Their INR prices, Hindi support and any gaming templates are unverified.
- Canva Magic Studio thumbnail and gaming-template features were not verified.
- No source confirmed any mainstream editor has auto-zoom-on-kill or facecam auto-layout. That could mean it is absent or just not documented.

## 5. All-in-one creator platforms and "creator OS" / agency tools

### Takeaway
Gaming-first clippers (Spikes Studio, Eklipse) and generalists (OpusClip) are adding team workspaces, brand templates, AI B-roll and prompt-based clipping. Approval workflows with roles live in social-media managers (Sprout, Hootsuite, Swat.io) and vidIQ Enterprise, not in gaming clippers. No tool found combines game-aware clipping, a content calendar, approve-then-post to YouTube, Hinglish captions and INR pricing. That combination is GCM's white space.

### Cited Findings
- **Spikes Studio**: aimed at streamers, with gaming-optimised detection using visual and audio signals and word-synced auto-captions. Prices conflict across third-party and competitor pages: Free; Basic/Starter $9.99–12.99; Pro+ $32.99 (300 min); Enterprise $54.99–120. One review says a 2-hour VOD uses the whole starter allowance, uploads are manual, and there is no mobile app. **No official page read.** — [Eklipse vs Spikes](https://eklipse.gg/compare/eklipse-vs-spikes-studio/) (competitor); [AutoClip comparison](https://autoclip.dev/blog/vizard-vs-spikes-studio-vs-autoclip-for-clippers-2026) (competitor); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/spikes-studio/pricing); [BityClips](https://bityclips.com/tool/spikes-studio/pricing)
- **OpusClip (new, beyond earlier report)**: **Team workspace** on Pro shares credits, videos and **brand templates**. Seats come in packs of 2, up to 10 (some localized pages say 4 users). Brand templates: 1 on the lower tier, up to 4 on the higher. **AI B-roll** "from 10+ sources". **ClipAnything** clips any genre and supports **natural-language prompts to clip a specific moment**. No dated 2026 changelog found. — [OpusClip team workspace](https://www.opus.pro/team-workspace); [OpusClip media & entertainment](https://opus.pro/business/media-and-entertainment); [OpusClip pricing (es)](https://www.opus.pro/es-es/pricing)
- **Approval and role tools**: Sprout Social offers content-approval workflows before posts go live, and Hootsuite offers role-based access and multi-user approvals for YouTube publishing. Swat.io markets team assignment and approvals. These come from listing or vendor pages, not verified docs. — [GitNux listing](https://gitnux.org/best/youtube-channel-management-software/); [Swat.io YouTube](https://swat.io/en/youtube/)
- **vidIQ Enterprise**: team roles and permissions, cross-channel reporting, for brands, agencies and MCNs. — [vidIQ pricing](https://vidiq.com/pricing/)
- **Indie YouTuber–editor collaboration tools** exist as portfolio projects (e.g. "You Collab"), with no evidence of traction. — [Contra](https://contra.com/p/LEbBPYPk-you-collab-you-tuber-and-editor-collaboration-platform)

### Inferences (feature inventory for GCM)
- **Table stakes** (most rivals or YouTube offer them; GCM must have them or link to them): auto-clipping long videos; 9:16 reframe; auto-captions with styles; export without watermark on paid plans; posting to Shorts and Reels; a basic calendar or schedule; brand template or preset; trim editor.
- **Free from YouTube, so don't build**: dubbing; title and thumbnail A/B tests; idea generation (Ask Studio); clipping from your own archived stream; collaborations.
- **Differentiators some have, but none for Indian mobile gaming**: game-event detection (Eklipse, Spikes, Streamlabs Sidekick, partly); prompt-based clip search (OpusClip ClipAnything); team workspaces (OpusClip Pro, vidIQ Enterprise); approval workflows (Sprout, Hootsuite, and Eklipse's Content Agent per the earlier report).
- **White space no one combines**: (1) BGMI/Free Fire HUD-aware moment ranking, plus (2) Roman-Hinglish captions and titles, plus (3) a facecam-plus-gameplay vertical layout with kill-zoom, plus (4) a creator/editor approval queue that posts to YouTube on schedule, plus (5) INR/UPI hours-based pricing, plus (6) clips judged against the creator's own past results. Each piece exists somewhere; the combination does not.
- Biggest substitution risks: YouTube's free live AI highlights (if it reaches desktop streams) and Streamlabs Ultra bundling clipping into a bill streamers already pay.

### Gaps
- No official Spikes Studio, Powder, Captions app or Medal pricing page read. Powder was not researched at all.
- Eklipse new features beyond the earlier report (e.g. Content Agent confirmation) were not re-checked.
- No Indian "creator OS" or agency platform with approvals was found. That suggests an absence but does not prove it.
- G2, Capterra and Product Hunt review data, and Indian tech press (Gadgets360, 91mobiles, Inc42) on these tools, were not consulted beyond what is cited.
