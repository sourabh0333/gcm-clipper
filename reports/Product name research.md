# Product name research: replacing "GCM Clipper"

Checks run on 6 November 2026. Decision: **Kaatclip** (D0 in the master plan).

## How the checks were done

- Domains: RDAP (`rdap.org`, re-confirmed at Verisign for .com and Google Registry for .app). A 404 means unregistered, a 200 means registered.
- YouTube handles: `youtube.com/@name`, with a random handle as the 404 baseline and `@eklipse` as the 200 control.
- Google Play: the store search page for the exact name.
- Web and companies: searches for "NAME app", "NAME clips", "NAME gaming", Tracxn and Crunchbase.
- Indian trademark: web search plus the registerkaro aggregator (the official IP India search needs a captcha, so this is not a legal clearance).
- Instagram: the profile page without login. A missing profile text is how a free handle renders, but this could not be confirmed through Instagram's API. Treat Instagram as "probably free, verify by hand".

## Candidates considered (28)

- Gaming slang: Clutchly, Clutchkat, Highlite, Knockd, Snipeclip, Peakcut, Hypecut
- Hinglish: Katai, Kaat, Kaatlo, Katlo, Kaatly, Kaatclip, Clipkaat, Clipkar, Clipwala, Klipwala, Shortwala, Jhatka, Jhatpat, Jhalak, Tukda, Tukdo, Dhamaka, Dhansu, Rapchik, Kadak, Bawaal, Tagda, Chhanto, Jhakaas
- Invented: Klipzo, Kaatz, Kaato, Kaatu
- Compound English: Tezclip, Todclip, Katclip, Kadakclip

Dropped early: Chakka (slur in Hindi); Dhamaka, Kadak, Bawaal, Rapchik, Jhakaas, Jhatka (domains registered, most YouTube handles taken); Tez- names (Google's former "Tez" payment brand).

## Decision: Kaatclip

Verified free on 6 November 2026: kaatclip.com (Verisign), kaatclip.in (rdap.org), kaatclip.app (Google Registry), @kaatclip on YouTube; no app on Google Play, no company or product, nothing on Tracxn or Crunchbase, no Indian trademark found in aggregators.

Why: "kaat" (cut) + "clip" says what the product does in Hinglish, reads in English, contains no game name, is nothing like Cevrynt, and is not embarrassing in Hindi, Tamil ("kaattu" = show or forest) or Telugu ("kaatu" = bite).

Not verified: kaatclip.gg (no RDAP for .gg), Instagram @kaatclip, the official IP India register. Owner tasks: register the three domains and the YouTube handle, confirm Instagram by hand, and have a lawyer glance at Katto and KatKut when filing the trademark in classes 9 and 42.
